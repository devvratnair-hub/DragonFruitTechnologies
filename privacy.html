Privacy notice

Your information, handled plainly.

Updated October 30, 2026.

Who we are

Dragonfruit Technologies is the independent, sole-proprietor consulting trade name of Devvrat Nair, based in Calgary, Alberta. This notice explains what happens to information when you visit this website or contact us through it.

What this website collects

This website has no contact forms, no live chat, no analytics and no advertising trackers. It does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser.

When you contact us

If you email, call or book a consultation, we use what you share (such as your name, contact details, organization and project requirements) only to reply, arrange conversations and prepare for an engagement.

Please keep first contact at project level. Don't send employee records, Payroll data, personal information about other people, or system credentials until an agreement and the client's security requirements are in place.

Services this website relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and receives the technical information needed to serve it, such as your IP address and browser details. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests font files from Google. See Google Fonts privacy information.
  • Calendly handles consultation booking. The booking link opens Calendly's site, where Calendly's privacy notice applies. Booked calls take place on Microsoft Teams, where Microsoft's privacy statement applies.
  • LinkedIn and other linked sites follow their own policies when you visit them.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails and booking details are kept only as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the records an engagement requires.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email devvrat.nair@dragonfruittechnologies.com. Information you gave directly to a service provider, such as Calendly or LinkedIn, may need a separate request to that provider.
